WhatsNew, a tracker for TV shows and movies

An app for Android and Android TV. Log what you watch, get an alert when the next episode comes out and share your list with someone else.

This page contains the app's privacy policy.

What the app does

Tracking
Mark what you have watched, pick up where you left off and see upcoming episodes in a home screen widget.
Alerts and calendar
Release notifications for the shows you follow, configurable in Settings.
Recommendations
Suggestions based on your ratings and the streaming services you subscribe to.
Profiles and Duo
Several profiles per account, with an optional PIN, and lists shared with your partner or family.
Statistics
Hours watched, genres and ratings, plus a year-in-review card.
Trakt and Stremio
Import your history from Trakt (.zip file) and Stremio (read-only), a link to open titles in Stremio and an optional addon.
Where to watch
Availability by service and country, with quick access to your streaming apps.
Android TV
Remote-friendly interface, picture-in-picture mode and a "Continue watching" row.

Privacy policy

We do not request location, contacts, camera or microphone permissions. We only use Internet, notifications (for release alerts) and vibration.

3. How we use the information

  • To provide the service: tracking, sync, calendar, recommendations and statistics.
  • To send you notifications about releases of the shows you follow, which you can configure or turn off in Settings.
  • To detect and fix errors, and to improve performance and security.

We do not sell your data and do not use it for advertising. The app shows no ads and does not use the advertising ID.

4. Analytics and diagnostics: your choice

The first time you open the app we ask whether you accept analytics. It is off by default until you accept, and you can change it at any time in Settings → Send usage analytics.

  • Essential (always): authentication and sync with Firebase, app integrity checks (App Check / Play Integrity) and remote configuration.
  • Optional: anonymous usage statistics (PostHog, EU servers, and Firebase Analytics), crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics) and performance measurement (Firebase Performance). They do not include profile names or personal data.

5. Who we share data with

We do not share your personal information with third parties except the providers needed to run the app, which act as processors:

  • Google Firebase / Google Play Services: authentication, cloud database, remote configuration, App Check and, with your consent, Analytics, Crashlytics and Performance.
  • PostHog (EU): anonymous usage statistics, only with consent.
  • The Movie Database (TMDB): source of title details, images and availability by service. Requests are made without sending your email or name. This app uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.
  • Trakt and Stremio: only if you choose to import or link your data.

Some providers may process data outside the European Economic Area under the safeguards provided by the GDPR (standard contractual clauses).

6. Security and retention

  • Encrypted communications (HTTPS) and access rules so that each user can only read their own data.
  • Integration keys are stored encrypted on the device.
  • We disable Android automatic backups so that your local data does not leave the device.
  • We keep your data while your account is active. When you delete it, the data is permanently erased.

7. Children

WhatsNew is not directed at children under 14 and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has given us data, write to us and we will delete it.

8. Your rights and how to exercise them

You may access, rectify, erase, restrict, object to and request portability of your data, and lodge a complaint with your data protection authority (AEPD in Spain).

  • Export your data: Settings → Export user data creates a copy of your preferences and watchlist.
  • Delete your account and all your data: Settings → Delete account. Your account, profiles, lists and history in the cloud, and the local data on the device, are erased. This cannot be undone.
  • No access to the app: write to d.noel.d.t.c@gmail.com from your account email and we will delete it.
  • Withdraw consent for analytics and diagnostics in Settings, whenever you want.
